Privacy Policy

The Emperor and His General

朕与山河皆负卿

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Introduction

MM Lucky Team operates The Emperor and His General, an Android novel and audiobook application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users access our application.

2. Application Features

The application provides fictional romance novels in seven languages:

English, Chinese, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia, Thai, Tamil and Filipino.

The application also provides optional text-to-speech narration.

3. Personal Information

Users do not need to create an account to read the novels.

The application does not require users to submit their names, phone numbers or email addresses to access the content.

4. Local Data Storage

Reading progress, bookmarks, language preferences, font settings, narration settings and applicable ad-free reward information are stored locally on the user's device.

Users can remove locally stored information by clearing application data or uninstalling the application.

5. Advertising - Google AdMob

This application uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, advertising interactions, diagnostics and related technical information.

Advertising data is handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

Users may be offered advertising consent choices where required.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data: Google Partner Sites Policy

6. Text-to-Speech Narration

The audiobook feature uses text-to-speech services available on the user's device.

Some speech engines may process novel text through online services depending on the selected voice provider and device settings.

The application does not require microphone access for text-to-speech narration.

We do not store narration recordings on our own servers.

7. Data Sharing

We do not operate a server that receives users' local reading history or bookmarks.

Third-party providers, including Google AdMob and text-to-speech providers, may process information according to their own policies.

8. Data Retention

Reading settings remain on the device until the user clears application data or uninstalls the application.

Third-party data retention is governed by the applicable provider's privacy policy.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for mature readers aged 18 and above.

It is not designed for children and does not knowingly request children's personal information.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit unnecessary information collection.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or third-party services change.
